Small on purpose.

Mini is Jaipur's boutique coworking space and creator studio. A curated community where you're known by name, your workday is handled, and a professional studio lets you build your brand alongside your business. Not a desk in a crowd — a home base that helps you grow.

Why Mini exists

In Jaipur, independents and small teams are stuck choosing between anonymous desk-farms and lonely home offices — with nowhere that builds their community, handles their day, and helps them grow a brand.

So we built the third option: a boutique, curated space with concierge service and a studio built in.

Our mission

Mini exists to give Jaipur's creators and small teams a home that knows them, handles their day, and helps them build their brand — not just a desk.

Our vision

A Jaipur where independent builders don't work alone or unseen — where the place you work also grows your network, your brand, and your business.

A note from the founder

Mini is founder-built, not franchise-imported — started by Devendra to be the room he wished existed in Jaipur: small enough to know everyone, serious enough to build in, with a studio so your work gets seen.

What "curated" means here

We cap the room on purpose and start every membership with a conversation. Not to be exclusive for its own sake — but because a community only works when the people in it are worth knowing. You join Mini the way you'd join a good dinner table: someone makes room, and someone introduces you.

The studio vision

Every independent builder is now also a publisher. Mini Studio exists so that the place you work can also grow your audience — record where you build, publish more, look bigger than you are.
